# LLM-Based Test Generation: Information Sources, Generation Strategies, and Quality Evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-15. Full 1,032-line / 12,871-word arXiv v1 read. Author survey repository checked. No experiment or model reproduction applies.

## Use in the project

Useful conceptual map for the thesis: separates test objectives/artifacts, information/provenance, generation mechanisms and quality evidence; distinguishes test artifact generation from evidence that an oracle captures expected behavior. It discusses bug reproduction, SWT-Bench and TC-Bench in the correct conceptual context, but uses none experimentally. It does not provide a root-cause taxonomy or evaluate the local Qwen model.

## Evidence and limits

Authors report a curated collection of 95 source records through 29 Sep 2026 (7 surveys/roadmaps + 88 primary records), but explicitly call it a structured narrative survey, not a completed systematic review. Most records have abstract/metadata inspection; selected records have full-text passages. Dual independent screening, complete variant-level coding, inter-rater validation, exhaustive database retrieval and quantitative synthesis remain pending. No experimental data, model results or reproduction are claimed. Treat its tables as a discovery map and verify claims from each cited primary paper.
