To address this limitation, we first construct two new benchmarks, Defects4J-Desc and Quixbugs-Desc, by augmenting the original benchmarks with NLDs. Then we propose DISTINCT, a novel LLM-based framework leveraging NLDs to guide the branch-consistency analysis, thereby revealing defects for focal methods.
Experiments on Defects4J-Desc and QuixBugs-Desc show that DISTINCT significantly improves DDR and other evaluation metrics, with robust generalization across different LLMs. These findings highlight the importance of description-guided and fault-aware test generation for improving practical software testing.
