# Revisiting Vul-RAG: Reproducibility and Replicability of RAG-based Vulnerability Detection with Open-Weight Models

Reviewed 2026-10-27; research only.

[Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04739v1).

2026 preprint reproduction. Reports limited pairwise discrimination: best tested overall pair accuracy 0.29 versus random 0.25, no consistent scale benefit. Selected models exclude some format-failing models. Results caution against expecting larger models or more retrieval iterations to resolve grounding. This is C/C++ vulnerability detection, not direct evidence about our Python task.
