## Context Management

Before context compaction:
- Preserve important technical decisions and their rationale.
- Preserve current task state and unresolved issues.
- Keep exact references to relevant files and APIs.
- Avoid losing constraints and edge cases.
- Maintain a checkpoint in CONTEXT.md when appropriate.

After compaction:
- Read CONTEXT.md when details are missing.
- Do not assume missing information.
