# When is Tree Search Useful for LLM Planning?

Reviewed 2026-10-14; research only. No reproduction, simulation or implementation started.

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10890)

Paper evaluates planning on text-to-SQL and mathematical reasoning, finding search usefulness strongly dependent on discriminator accuracy and potentially high runtime overhead. Numerical thresholds from those tasks are not universal requirements for LineFacts. Our proposal gives concrete execution/constraint evidence more weight than LM self-judgement.
