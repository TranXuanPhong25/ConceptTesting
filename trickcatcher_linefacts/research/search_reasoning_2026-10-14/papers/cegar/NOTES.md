# Counterexample-guided Abstraction Refinement

Reviewed 2026-10-14; research only. No reproduction, simulation or implementation started.

[Primary source](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~emc/papers/Conference%20Papers/Counterexample-guided%20Abstraction%20Refinement.pdf)

The classic work iteratively refines abstractions when an apparent counterexample is spurious. It supports the design principle of starting coarse and adding relevant predicates after concrete checking. Our proposed finite relation-class testing is not a sound implementation of the full CEGAR/model-checking framework; correctness claims must retain scope and abstraction assumptions.
